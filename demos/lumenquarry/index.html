The craft behind the wall

Every job starts by understanding the space and how it's used. The work that follows should be careful, organized, and explained plainly, so you know what was done.

Before you call

What should I mention when I call?

Whether it's a home or a business, what you're seeing or what you'd like added, and roughly when it started. Photos of the area can help in later conversations.

A breaker keeps tripping. What should I do?

Note which circuit and what was running when it tripped, and avoid repeatedly resetting it. Then call to describe what's happening.

Is there anything I shouldn't try myself?

Opening a panel or working on wiring can be dangerous. If you see smoke, sparking, or scorch marks, or smell burning, keep clear and call 911 first.
