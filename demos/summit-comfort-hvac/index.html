How we work

A straight answer, every visit.

HVAC problems can have several possible causes. That's why we don't diagnose over the phone or quote a price before we've seen your system. Here's what to expect instead.

  1. Tell us what's happening

    Call or send a request. We'll ask only what we need — system type, symptoms, and when it started.

  2. We confirm your appointment

    Our office confirms the next available window with you directly. We never promise a time we can't keep.

  3. A technician evaluates in person

    A qualified technician inspects the equipment and finds the actual cause before recommending anything.

  4. You choose, with clear options

    We explain what we found, what it will take, and your choices — repair or replace — in plain language.

Common questions

Good questions, honest answers.

Do you service my area?+

We serve Jefferson City, Holts Summit, Columbia, Fulton, California, Ashland, and surrounding communities within about 40 miles of Jefferson City. If you're outside that area, contact us and we'll confirm availability first.

How much will my repair cost?+

It depends on the specific problem and your equipment, so we don't guess over the phone. A technician evaluates the system first, and our team confirms pricing with you before any work begins.

Can someone come out today?+

We'll do our best to help quickly, but we won't promise a time we haven't confirmed. Send a request or call, and our office will confirm the next available appointment.

My AC is running but blowing warm air. What's wrong?+

That symptom can have several possible causes. A technician would need to evaluate the system to know for sure. When you contact us, let us know the system type, its approximate age, and any unusual sounds or smells.

Do you offer emergency service?+

Yes — 24/7 for existing customers and homeowners with a complete loss of heating or cooling.

Do you install new systems?+

Yes. We install residential air conditioners, furnaces, and heat pumps, and we'll walk you through options that fit your home before you decide.

Is my repair covered by warranty?+

Coverage depends on your equipment and its warranty terms. Share your system details and our team will help confirm what applies.
