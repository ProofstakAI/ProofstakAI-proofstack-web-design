04 / BEFORE YOU ASK

A few good
questions.

Are these real client websites?+

No. All eight, including Summit Comfort HVAC, are working concept builds with fictional business identities and contact details. They demonstrate design and functionality, not client relationships or measured results.

Do I need a video-heavy website?+

No. This site is a creative showcase. Your website should use the right tools for your audience, whether that's a cinematic opening or a fast, focused service page. Readability, accessibility, and performance come first.

Can you connect AI or other business tools?+

Yes, that can be part of a separately scoped Proofstack project. We first agree on what connects, what data it can access, and which actions need human approval.

What does a project cost?+

A straightforward launch site (home, services, portfolio, contact) starts at $500 and takes 1–2 weeks. A fuller custom site with original photography, copywriting, and integrations typically runs $2,500–$6,000 over 3–5 weeks. A premium interactive build like the ones in our portfolio starts around $7,500 and runs 5–8 weeks. Every quote includes a build timeline and a set number of revision rounds, agreed before work starts.

What happens after launch?+

You own the domain, hosting account, and code from day one. Ongoing updates are available on a simple monthly care plan or billed per request, whichever fits how often you expect to change things. Hosting itself typically runs a few dollars a month through providers like Netlify; we help you set that account up in your own name.
